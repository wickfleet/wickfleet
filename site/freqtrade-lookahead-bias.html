Check a Freqtrade strategy for lookahead bias

A backtest with lookahead bias uses candles the bot could not have seen at the time. The result looks excellent, and the running bot never repeats it. This page covers where the bias comes from, how to find it with Freqtrade's own tools, and how Wickfleet runs those checks before a bot starts.

Where it comes from

  • A negative shift such as shift(-1), which reads the next candle
  • A statistic over the whole column, such as dataframe["close"].mean(). In a backtest the column holds the future too
  • .iloc[-1] inside the populate functions, which is the last candle of the whole backtest, not the current one
  • A higher timeframe merged by hand with .merge(). Candle dates are open times, so a plain merge hands the 1h candle to the 5m rows before it has closed. merge_informative_pair aligns on the close

Freqtrade's own checks

freqtrade lookahead-analysis --strategy MyStrategy --timerange 20250101-20260101
freqtrade recursive-analysis --strategy MyStrategy --timerange 20250101-20260101
CommandFinds
lookahead-analysissignals that change when the data after them is cut away. It needs trades to check, 10 by default (--minimum-trade-amount), so a strategy that barely trades cannot be judged
recursive-analysisindicator values that depend on how much history was loaded, which makes a backtest and a running bot disagree. It checks one pair, the first in the whitelist unless -p names another, and Freqtrade recommends a range of at least 5000 candles

What Wickfleet does with them

A replay in Wickfleet is a backtest of one bot on its real config, so fees and stake match the bot. After the backtest, two more steps run on the same data.

CheckResult
Lookahead analysis, run with a minimum of 5 tradesa bias blocks a new bot with those settings
Too few trades to judgeshown as a warning
Recursive analysis, drift above 0.1% at the strategy's own startupshown as a warning

Beyond bias: the robustness verdict

A backtest without bias can still be luck. Every replay also gets a verdict from its own trades, with no extra run.

CheckPass rule (defaults)
Walk-forward windowsthe range splits into 30-day windows. At least 6
Totalnet profit over the replay above 0
Consistencyat least half the windows profitable
Worst windowno window below -15%
Monte Carlo10,000 resamples of the trades. At most 5% reach a 50% drawdown. Under 20 trades it is shown, not judged

A failed verdict warns on a dry-run bot. It is the gate for live trading: saving a bot with real money through the hub needs your own replay of exactly its settings from the last 7 days, with no lookahead bias found and this verdict passed.

A clean check does not make a strategy good. It says the backtest did not cheat, nothing more.

Try it

git clone https://github.com/wickfleet/wickfleet.git
cd wickfleet
make quickstart

Needs Docker with Compose v2, git and make, on macOS or Linux. Everything runs on one machine, bound to 127.0.0.1, and a dry-run demo bot is waiting in the fleet. More in the install guide, or look first at the read-only demo.
