Fleet risk

One page for what the fleet holds right now: open trades, capital at stake, exposure per coin and per pair, drawdown and stoploss distance, read live from each bot.

What you see

SectionMeaning
At stakestake in open trades, per stake currency, against the bots' wallets
Botsopen trades against the limit, stake, drawdown now against the worst so far
Exposure per coinone line per base coin across pairs and bots. The amount sums, the value stays per currency
Exposure per pairstake per pair across bots and its share of the total
Open trades and their stopseach open trade with its profit and the distance to its stoploss
Pair overlapconfigured pairs that two or more bots trade, so one move hits several bots

Where the numbers come from

  • The hub reads /status, /profit and /balance of each bot you may see, and caches them for 30 s
  • Money is summed per stake currency, and dry and live are never added together. A USDT bot and a BTC bot stay two lines
  • Drawdown is freqtrade's current_drawdown against max_drawdown over closed trades
  • A bot that does not answer is listed with its error and left out of every sum

Who sees what

Admins see every bot, operators their own. Viewers and demo visitors get counts and percentages, with no stake, value or exposure lists.
