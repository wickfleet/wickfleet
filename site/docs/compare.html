Compare bots

Pick two or more bots and a date range. Wickfleet replays each one over the same window, with its own pairs, strategy, timeframe and stake, and lines the results up.

What you see

ColumnFrom
Profit, buy and holdfreqtrade's backtest result for the bot's own config, fees included
Max drawdownfreqtrade's max_relative_drawdown
Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, profit factorfreqtrade's own figures. Left out when freqtrade has none
Win rate, trades, average holdthe backtest result
Windowswalk-forward: the range in 30-day windows, how many were profitable
MC DD (95%)Monte Carlo: 10,000 resamples of the trades, the drawdown 95% of them stay under
Verdictthe robustness verdict of the replay: pass, fail, or biased when the lookahead check found a bias
Return curvestart balance plus each trade's net profit on the day it closed, one point per day

How it runs

  • Each bot is an ordinary replay job: download-data into the shared candle store, then backtesting, then freqtrade's lookahead and recursive analysis
  • The comparison queues one bot at a time, so it never holds more than one replay slot. At most 2 comparisons run at once and 12 bots go in one
  • The newest 50 comparisons are kept on disk, so a restart or a read-only demo still shows them
  • A bot whose replay fails is listed with its error, never with zeros

Who sees what

RoleCompare
adminall comparisons, with money
operatortheir own bots, with money
viewer, demopercentages, ratios, counts and verdicts. Balances, absolute profit and equity in currency are removed on the server, the curve stays as return %

A comparison describes the past. A bot that wins over one window can lose over the next, which is why the walk-forward and Monte Carlo columns sit next to the profit.
